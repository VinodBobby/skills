# Investment Research

Compare investments against the user's portfolio policy and account constraints. Use investor styles as optional research lenses, not as substitutes for evidence or the user's goals.

## Workflow

1. Establish the mandate, account eligibility, investable universe, desired exposure, time horizon, restrictions, and data date. Include existing holdings and overlapping exposures when available. If material constraints are unknown, label the comparison conditional.
2. Compare suitable funds or other vehicles using current prospectuses, issuer disclosures, and available market data. Explain the index or strategy, holdings and concentration, risks, liquidity, distributions, tracking where relevant, and availability in each account.
3. Audit total costs: expense ratio for the actual share class, loads, account or advisory fees, spreads and trading costs when relevant, and the tax and transaction effects of a change. Verify minimums and share-class eligibility; a lower expense ratio alone does not establish a better choice.
4. If the user asks for a named investor's approach, read [the profile method](references/investor-profile-method.md) and select the relevant profile from [the catalog](references/investor-profiles/index.md) or a user-supplied private source. For a private repository or directory, follow [the private source guidance](references/private-profile-sources.md). Create a sourced profile when needed. Apply documented principles as explicit tests or questions; label adaptations and evidence gaps.
5. Present a comparison, the basis for any ranking, sources and as-of dates, uncertainties, and the effect on existing portfolio exposures. Include a no-change option when reviewing an existing holding. Carry the run and snapshot identifiers forward when part of a coordinated review.

For individual-company fundamentals, use the existing equity-research skill when available, or perform equivalent sourced due diligence. A style lens does not establish what a person currently owns, would buy, or would recommend.

## Requirements

Current comparisons require current issuer documents, prices, and account availability. When these are unavailable, use dated supplied data and identify the missing checks. Do not invent fees, returns, holdings, profiles, or citations. Research and proposals do not authorize account changes or orders.
