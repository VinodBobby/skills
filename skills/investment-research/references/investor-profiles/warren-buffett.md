# Warren Buffett

## Scope

Buffett's business-investing approach at Berkshire Hathaway; sources checked 2026-11-08. The record below is Berkshire's company share performance, not a separately audited personal stock-picking account.

## Documented method

- Buffett described a preference for a strong business at a fair price over a weaker business bought very cheaply. [Berkshire's 1989 letter](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1989.html)
- He described durable competitive advantages and the risk that a perceived advantage may erode. [Berkshire's 2007 letter](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2007ltr.pdf?v=1675364079773)

## Observed record

Berkshire reports a 19.7% compounded annual gain in its per-share market value for 1965–2025, compared with 10.5% for the S&P 500 including dividends. Its table also reports a 48.7% decline in Berkshire's per-share market value in 1974. The early reporting periods include fiscal-year exceptions. [Berkshire 2025 annual report, performance table](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/2025ar/2025ar.pdf)

The company owns operating businesses and investments and has benefited from many contributors. Its share-price record is not an individual's fund return; investor taxes and comparable fund fees are not specified in the table. The period and vehicle are not directly comparable with the other profiles.

## Limits and open questions

The approach evolved over time, and Berkshire's opportunities, financing, and scale differ from a household portfolio. Recheck current holdings and disclosures separately; do not infer a present recommendation from this historical profile.
