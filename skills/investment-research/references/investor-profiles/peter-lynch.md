# Peter Lynch

## Scope

Lynch managed Fidelity Magellan from 1977 to 1990. Sources checked 2026-11-08. [Fidelity fund history](https://www.fidelity.com/myfidelity/InsideFidelity/NewsCenter/quickFacts/Magellan.html)

## Documented method

Familiarity is an idea source, not evidence that a stock is attractively priced. ## Observed record

A 2008 San Francisco Chronicle table, citing the independent Fidelity Investor newsletter, reports a 29.2% average annual return for Magellan from May 1977 to May 1990. [Historical manager table](https://www.sfgate.com/business/article/fidelity-magellan-fund-reopens-to-investors-3298154.php)

The table does not specify a matched benchmark, fee and tax treatment, reinvestment assumptions, currency, or drawdown. Treat the figure as a reported fund record with those gaps, not a verified personal investor return or a basis for forecasting future results.
