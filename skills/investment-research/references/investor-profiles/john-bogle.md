# John Bogle

## Scope

Bogle founded Vanguard and championed low-cost index investing. Vanguard says its 500 Index Fund launched in 1976; sources checked 2026-11-08. [Vanguard history](https://corporate.vanguard.com/content/corporatesite/us/en/corp/articles/50-years-50-facts-indexing-since-1976.html)

## Documented method

Vanguard describes Bogle's emphasis on broad market exposure, low costs, and a long-term approach. An index fund seeks to follow a benchmark rather than select stocks to beat it. [Vanguard on indexing](https://corporate.vanguard.com/indexing)

## Observed record

Vanguard reports that Investor Shares of its 500 Index Fund returned an annualized 11.44% from inception through March 31, 2026, versus 11.68% for the S&P 500 over the same period. [Vanguard's historical account](https://corporate.vanguard.com/content/corporatesite/us/en/corp/articles/50-years-50-facts-indexing-since-1976.html)

This is a fund and benchmark record, not Bogle's personal return: other people managed the fund, and much of the period followed his tenure. The fund figure is a total return; the cited fund/benchmark comparison does not specify individual investor taxes or drawdown. Confirm fee and reinvestment conventions in the underlying performance documents before comparing it with a different vehicle.

## Research lens

Adaptation: compare broad, eligible index vehicles on the exposure they actually provide, full costs, tracking, and fit with the user's asset allocation. A broad U.S. equity index alone does not establish diversification across every asset class or geography. Consider available alternatives rather than assuming one provider or share class is appropriate.

## Limits and open questions

The fund and its benchmark both bear market risk. The cited history is a strategy example, not evidence that Bogle personally managed the fund throughout the period or would endorse a current recommendation.
