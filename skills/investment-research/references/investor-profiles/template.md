# Investor name

## Scope

Identify the person, role, vehicle or mandate, period studied, and date the sources were checked.

## Documented method

Summarize source-backed principles and observed behavior. Link evidence near each material claim and distinguish any changes by period.

## Observed record

Identify the vehicle and period; return type and annualization; comparable benchmark; fees, taxes, distributions and reinvestment; currency; and risk or drawdown when available. Cite the source and mark unverified fields as unknown. Explain attribution limits.

## Research lens

List practical questions or tests adapted from the documented method. Label inference and any chosen thresholds; explain where the lens may not fit the user's policy.

## Limits and open questions

Record counterexamples, incomplete disclosures, conflicting sources, and the next evidence needed. Do not imply that historical performance will recur or that the person endorses a proposed investment.
