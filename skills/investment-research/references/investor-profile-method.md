# Investor profile method

Use a profile to apply documented investment principles as a research lens. Describe the person and period being studied; do not impersonate the person or present a hypothetical conclusion as their current view.

## Research or update a profile

1. Define the person, roles, vehicles, mandate, periods, and research date. Check the [catalog](investor-profiles/index.md) for an existing profile and verify time-sensitive claims before reuse.
2. Prefer the person's own letters, interviews, filings, prospectuses, and audited or official fund reports. Use credible independent sources where primary records are unavailable. Cite each material claim and distinguish the person's stated method, observed behavior, and your interpretation. Note changes in style across periods and material counterexamples.
3. Describe a historical record only for a specifically identified fund, portfolio, company, or strategy and period. Report return type and annualization, benchmark over a matching period, fees, taxes, reinvestment, currency, risk or drawdown, and source where available. Mark unknown fields; never fill gaps with assumptions or attribute a firm's results solely to one person.
4. Treat public holdings disclosures as dated and incomplete. For example, U.S. Form 13F does not show every security or short position. Do not infer a present holding, trade date, portfolio weight, thesis, or personal endorsement from a filing alone.
5. Translate supported principles into practical research questions or tests. Label each translation as an adaptation, state where it may not fit the user's policy or account, and include disconfirming evidence. Avoid invented numerical thresholds and claims that the approach can reproduce the person's returns.

When the user asks to add a reusable profile to this package, follow the [template](investor-profiles/template.md) and link it from the catalog. For a user-supplied private location, follow [the private source guidance](private-profile-sources.md). Otherwise provide the profile in the requested output without changing either catalog. Include source dates, limitations, and what needs further verification.
