# Purpose

Build an evidence-backed stock screen or company analysis that lets the reader trace each important conclusion to its source and assumptions.

# Workflow

## Set the scope

Identify the market or universe, screening criteria, comparison group, reporting currency, and analysis date. Use the user's criteria. If a missing choice would materially change the candidates, ask; otherwise state a reasonable assumption before screening.

Separate hard filters from ranking criteria. Record thresholds, formulas, reporting periods, and data cut-off dates so another analyst can reproduce the screen.

## Scan candidates

Use current market data and recent company information. Prefer regulatory filings, audited reports, earnings releases, exchange disclosures, and company investor-relations materials for company facts. Use independent financial reporting for context. Treat search snippets and aggregator summaries as leads, not evidence.

For every metric, record its value, units, period, as-of date, and source. Label reported results, company-adjusted measures, management guidance, analyst estimates, and your own calculations separately. Check that peer comparisons use compatible periods and definitions; explain when they do not.

Return the screened universe, filters applied, candidates that pass, and any missing data that affected inclusion. Show the criteria behind rankings instead of hiding them in a composite score.

## Drill into finalists

For each company, examine its business and revenue drivers; growth and margins; cash generation and conversion; debt, liquidity, and dilution; capital allocation; competitive and regulatory exposure; and material catalysts and risks.

Compare valuation with relevant peers and the company's history. Use a discounted cash-flow model only when its assumptions can be stated and sourced. Show key inputs and sensitivity to assumptions. Check for material changes, restatements, unusual adjustments, customer or product concentration, and differences between earnings and cash flow.

## Report findings

Give a concise comparison table and a sourced deep dive for the requested finalists. Distinguish verified facts from estimates and interpretation. Include the strongest counterevidence, unresolved questions, and what new fact could change the conclusion. State the data date and source coverage.

Keep analysis separate from a trading decision. Do not present a screen, valuation, or forecast as a guaranteed outcome or personalized recommendation.

# Requirements

Current screens require access to up-to-date market data and source documents. Use available search, browsing, financial-data, or user-provided tools. If live data is unavailable, analyze supplied data with its date clearly stated and identify what could not be verified; never invent prices, financials, estimates, or citations.
